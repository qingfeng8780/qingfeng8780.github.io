Xiangye Zhu 朱相烨

Academic homepage

Learning from sensor data
to understand physical systems.

My research focuses on multimodal sensing, temporal modeling, and physics-guided machine learning. I work with acoustic emission, vibration, and force signals to study physical processes through data and mechanistic models.

I received my M.Eng. in Electronic Science and Technology from Wuhan University in June 2026, supervised by Xiaohu Xu, and my B.Eng. in Automation from Wuhan University in 2023. My graduate research investigated sensor-based prediction of wear and surface quality in robotic manufacturing.

I am interested in extending this experience to multimodal perception and learning for robotic interaction.

Research

From physical measurements to learned models

Multimodal sensing

Processing acoustic emission, vibration, and force signals to extract complementary information about physical processes.

Signal processing · Feature fusion

Temporal modeling

Learning relationships across time and sensor modalities to estimate wear and surface quality under changing conditions.

Bidirectional LSTM · Transformers

Physics-guided learning

Combining mechanistic predictions with learned residuals to connect physical knowledge and data-driven models.

Hybrid models · Residual learning
Experimental background

Graduate research on an established robotic grinding platform, with study design, algorithm implementation, experiments, quantitative evaluation, and manuscript preparation.
