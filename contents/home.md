#### About

My research focuses on **multimodal sensing, temporal modeling, and physics-guided machine learning** for robotic and physical systems. I have worked with acoustic emission, vibration, and force signals to investigate wear and surface quality in robotic manufacturing.

I am seeking **PhD opportunities in robotics and machine learning**, and am interested in extending my experience to multimodal perception and learning for robotic interaction.
